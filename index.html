Clinical Reference Guide

Systematic methodology for monitoring and mitigating cognitive fatigue.

Standard Operating Procedure

1

Baseline Diagnostics (MDFI)

Begin by completing the Multidimensional Digital Fatigue Inventory in the "Assessment" tab. This generates your initial Composite Cognitive Score, establishing a clinical baseline for your attention span, impulsivity, and somatic disruption.

2

Telemetry Logging

Access the "Data Logger" tab daily to input your estimated screen time across major algorithmic platforms. Consistent logging allows the system to calculate your Daily Mean Volume and Routine Volatility Index (σ) accurately.

3

Behavioral Interventions

Navigate to the "Interventions" tab to execute evidence-based protocols. These are designed to systematically replace passive digital consumption with active, restorative analog habits, thereby lowering your cognitive fatigue.

4

Data Governance & Privacy

All recorded telemetry is stored securely via Firebase Cloud. You can access your data across devices by logging in. Use the "Data Management" tab to safely export your JSON/CSV dataset for external clinical review.
